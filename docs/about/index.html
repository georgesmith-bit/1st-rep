About Live2048

Free online puzzle games · no download, no sign-up

Live2048 is home to two free puzzle games built around the same satisfying slide-and-merge mechanic. Both run instantly in your browser — on desktop, mobile, or tablet — with no download and no account required.

▶ Play Classic 2048 🦄 Play Unicorn Merge

Classic 2048

The original number puzzle: slide tiles on a 4×4 grid, merge matching numbers, and reach the 2048 tile — then keep going toward 4096 and beyond. Learn the rules in our how-to-play guide, or level up with our advanced tips.

Unicorn Merge

Our cuter take on the same mechanic. Instead of numbers, you merge adorable pets, evolving them step by step until you reach the legendary 🦄 Unicorn. Every new pet you discover joins your collection. Same rules, new reward.

Why we made it

2048 is a timeless puzzle — five minutes to learn, weeks to master. We built Live2048 to make it instantly accessible, add a fresh pet-collecting twist, and keep the whole thing free. If you enjoy it, share it with a friend.
