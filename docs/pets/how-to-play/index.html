How to Play Unicorn Merge — Merge Pets from Slime to Unicorn

Last updated: September 2026 · 5 min read

Unicorn Merge is a charming merge puzzle: slide adorable pets on a 4×4 grid, merge two identical pets to evolve them into the next creature, and climb the evolution ladder all the way to the legendary 🦄 Unicorn. If you've played 2048, you already know the rules — here, numbers become pets and the reward is a full collection to discover.

1. Quick Start: How a Game Works

You start with two pets (usually two Slimes) on a 4×4 grid. Each move:

  1. Swipe (or press an arrow key) in one direction — up, down, left, or right.
  2. All pets slide as far as they can in that direction.
  3. When two identical pets collide, they merge into one and evolve into the next pet in the chain.
  4. A new pet appears in a random empty spot.

The goal: keep merging until you evolve your pets all the way to the 🦄 Unicorn. Along the way, every new pet you discover is added to your collection book.

💡 Pro Tip: Every merge adds to your score and — more importantly — a merge into a pet you've never seen before reveals it in your collection. Discovery is the heart of the game.

2. The Evolution Chain (12 Pets)

Every pet has a place on the ladder. Merge two of the same to evolve one step up:

4. Core Strategy: The Corner Method

The same strategy that wins 2048 wins Unicorn Merge:

  1. Pick a corner — most players use the bottom-right.
  2. Keep your rarest pet locked in that corner. Never move it out.
  3. Build descending pets along the bottom row: rarest pet in the corner, next-rarest beside it.
  4. Only use 2 directions — typically down and right. Avoid up unless you have to.
⚠️ Common Mistake: Swiping in all four directions randomly. This scatters your rare pets across the board and you'll run out of space fast. Discipline yourself to use only 2 directions 90% of the time.

5. Top 5 Tips

  1. Never swipe up unless the board is completely stuck. Up is the most dangerous direction — it can lift your rare pets out of position.
  2. Fill the bottom row first. Pets on the bottom don't move when you swipe down, so it's the safest home for your rare pets.
  3. Merge only when it feeds the chain. A merge in the top corner might feel good, but if it doesn't build your bottom-row ladder, it's just clutter.
  4. Watch for traps. If three sides of your rarest pet are blocked, you're one bad move from losing it. Plan 2–3 moves ahead.
  5. Check your collection. The collection book shows every pet you've discovered — a great motivator to keep climbing the ladder.

6. FAQ

Is Unicorn Merge free to play?

Yes — 100% free, no downloads, no sign-up. It runs in your browser on mobile and desktop.

How do I get the Unicorn?

Merge two Dragons together. Each Dragon is a very high-level tile, so it takes patience — but the Unicorn is the rarest pet of all, worth every merge.

How many pets are there?

12 pets in total, from the humble Slime to the legendary Unicorn. Each one is a step on the evolution ladder.

Is Unicorn Merge the same as 2048?

Same rules, new skin. It's the exact slide-and-merge puzzle, with pets instead of numbers — a fresh way to enjoy a classic.
