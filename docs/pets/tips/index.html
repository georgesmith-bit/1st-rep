Unicorn Merge Tips & Strategy — Reach the Unicorn Faster

Last updated: September 2026 · 5 min read

You've mastered the basics — now let's get you to the 🐉 Dragon and, eventually, the legendary 🦄 Unicorn. These are the strategies that separate casual swipers from players who complete the collection.

🦄 Play Unicorn Merge 📖 How to Play

1. The Corner Method, Mastered

Every top player uses one rule: your rarest pet lives in a corner and never leaves. The rest is discipline.

  1. Choose your corner early — bottom-right is the most popular.
  2. Build a descending chain along the bottom row: [Dragon][Tiger][Shark][Eagle] in order.
  3. Use only two directions (down + right). Resist the urge to swipe up.
  4. If your rarest pet gets dislodged, stop everything and put it back before merging anything else.
💡 Pro Tip: The bottom row is your anchor. Pets on the bottom row don't move when you swipe down, so it's the safest place for your most valuable pets.

2. How to Reach the Dragon Faster

The Dragon is a 2048-level pet — it needs serious organization to reach. Here's the fast path:

  • Never merge "for fun." Every merge should either build your chain or clear space for the chain.
  • Keep the top rows clean. Clutter in the top rows blocks the pets you actually need.
  • Prioritize the second-rarest pet. To make a Dragon you need two Peacocks, which need two Crocodiles each — work the ladder bottom-up, steadily.
⚠️ Common Mistake: Chasing a single big merge at the expense of the ladder. A juicy Peacock + Peacock merge in the top-left feels great but leaves your chain broken. Merge toward your corner, always.

3. Surviving the Late Game

When the board is nearly full, the game tests your patience:

  1. Slow down. One wrong swipe can scatter a pet you spent 50 moves building.
  2. Use undo as a learning tool. Try a move, watch the result, and rewind if it broke your chain.
  3. Accept small merges. Sometimes a Cat + Cat is the right move to free up space, even if it doesn't feel exciting.

4. Complete Your Collection

The collection book is the game's real treasure. Every pet you evolve into for the first time gets added automatically.

  • Discovering happens on merge. Merge into a new pet and it's recorded — even if you later lose the game.
  • The Slime is the easiest to miss. It's your starting pet, so it's recorded the moment you begin.
  • Track your progress with the collection button in the top bar — seeing "9/12" is a great push to keep going.

5. FAQ

What's the fastest way to reach the Dragon?

The corner method: lock your rarest pet in a corner, build a descending chain, and use only two directions. Organization beats luck.

Can I reach the Unicorn every game?

No — the Unicorn needs two Dragons, which is a long-term goal. Most games can reach the Dragon; the Unicorn is the reward for consistency.

What do I do when the board is almost full?

Protect your rarest pet, merge only toward your chain, and use undo to test risky moves before committing.

Does undo hurt my score?

No. Undo restores your previous move and score. Use it freely to learn.
