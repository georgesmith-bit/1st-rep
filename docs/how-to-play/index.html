1. Quick Start: How a Game Works

You start with two tiles (either two 2s or a 2 and a 4) on a 4×4 grid. Each move:

  1. You swipe (or press an arrow key) in one direction — up, down, left, or right.
  2. All tiles slide as far as they can in that direction.
  3. When two tiles with the same number collide, they merge into one tile with their sum.
  4. A new tile (2 or 4) appears in a random empty spot.

The goal: merge tiles until you reach 2048. But you can keep playing beyond 2048 — the real challenge is reaching 4096, 8192, or higher.

💡 Pro Tip: Each merge adds to your score. The score shown at the top tracks the sum of all merged values, not just the highest tile.

3. Core Strategy: The Corner Method

The #1 strategy used by every top 2048 player:

  1. Pick a corner — most players use the bottom-right.
  2. Keep your highest tile locked in that corner. Never move it out.
  3. Build descending values along the bottom row: high tile in corner, then 1024, 512, 256 next to it.
  4. Only use 2 directions — typically down and right. Avoid up unless absolutely necessary.
⚠️ Common Mistake: Using all four directions randomly. This scatters your high tiles across the board and you'll run out of space fast. Discipline yourself to use only 2 directions 90% of the time.

4. Step-by-Step Strategy for Beginners

Opening (tiles 2–128)

In the early game, focus on building a single high tile in your chosen corner. Don't worry about score yet — just keep merging toward the corner. Swipe down and right almost exclusively.

Midgame (tiles 128–1024)

Now maintain the descending chain along the bottom row. The top rows should mostly be empty or low numbers. If your high tile gets dislodged from the corner, prioritize getting it back before building anything else.

Endgame (1024–2048)

Your bottom row should look like: [2048][1024][512][256]. Only merge tiles that fit into this pattern. Avoid merging random tiles in the top rows — they create clutter that blocks your key tiles.

5. Top 5 Tips

  1. Never swipe up unless the board is completely stuck. Up is the most dangerous direction — it can lift your high tiles out of position.
  2. Fill the bottom row first. Tiles on the bottom don't move when you swipe down, so it's the safest home for your high-value tiles.
  3. Merge only when it helps the chain. A 4+4→8 merge in the top-left corner might feel good, but if it doesn't feed your bottom-row chain, it's just clutter.
  4. Watch for "trap" situations. If three sides of your high tile are blocked, you're one bad move from losing it. Plan 2-3 moves ahead.
  5. Undo is your training wheel. Use the undo button (Ctrl+Z) to experiment with different moves. Over time, you'll learn which moves lead to dead ends.

6. FAQ

Can I win 2048 every time?

No — randomness plays a role. Even the best strategy wins about 70-80% of the time. The key is consistency: minimize bad moves and you'll reach 2048 in most games.

What's the highest possible tile?

Theoretically 131,072 (217), assuming perfect play and perfect luck. In practice, the community record is 65,536. For most players, reaching 4096 is a major achievement.

Is there a solver or AI for 2048?

Yes — AI algorithms can reach 2048 over 90% of the time and 4096 about 10% of the time. But where's the fun in that? The joy of 2048 is in the struggle.

Does the undo button affect my score?

No. Undo simply rewinds your last move. Your score resets to what it was before that move. Use it freely to learn.
